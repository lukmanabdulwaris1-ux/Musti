Our Story

Passion Woven Into Every Stitch

HMK Tailor was founded with a single vision — to bring world-class tailoring to Lagos. We combine traditional craftsmanship with modern fashion sensibilities to create outfits that tell your story.

Every piece we create is a reflection of our commitment to quality, precision, and the unique personality of our clients.

  • ✦ 100% hand-measured and custom-fitted
  • ✦ Premium fabrics sourced locally and internationally
  • ✦ Delivery within 7–14 working days
  • ✦ Free alterations within 30 days
